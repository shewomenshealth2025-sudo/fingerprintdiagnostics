PACK 1 — Public website

PACK 3 — Patient portal
PACK 4 — Clinician portal

DEPLOYMENT
1. Delete the existing repository contents or create a clean repository.
2. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
3. Upload every extracted file directly into the repository root.
4. Do not upload the ZIP itself.
5. Confirm that index.html, styles.css, pack2.css, pack3.css, pack4.css and pack5.css are all in the root.
6. Commit the upload.
7. Wait 1–2 minutes and hard-refresh with Ctrl + Shift + R.

PROTOTYPE SAFETY
All people, records, scores and clinical data are fictional demonstration content.
Do not use real patient data or make clinical decisions from this prototype.
