About

A new framework for understanding disease.

Fingerprint Diagnostics is an early-stage health-technology concept exploring how combinations of biological signals may provide a richer picture than any single biomarker alone.

What is a ridge?

A ridge is a measurable biological or clinical feature that contributes to a wider disease pattern. It might represent inflammation, hormone activity, microbiome composition, symptoms, imaging features or another meaningful signal.

What is a fingerprint?

A fingerprint is the weighted combination of those ridges. The goal is to make that combination interpretable, visual and suitable for comparison with validated disease models.

Core principles

Explainable

Every output should show which signals contributed and why.

Evidence-based

Scientific confidence and evidence quality should always be visible.

Modular

The system should grow from one condition to many without being rebuilt.

Responsible

The platform is designed to support research and future clinical validation.
